## Version principale (EN)

How shift works, in 2 minutes.

Every token on shift keeps one address, one supply and every holder's balance for life. What changes is its identity: name, ticker, image.

In the video:
→ Mirage was born Chameleon, became Mimic, then Mirage. Same contract.
→ Glitch lets its holders vote on what it becomes next.
→ Larva molted on schedule. The keeper did it.
→ Echo mirrors whatever is trending.

Every rewrite is one on-chain transaction, logged forever. The creator never pays gas to shift.

Launch yours in one transaction: https://shift-spl.xyz/lab

## Réponse suggérée (thread)

Under the hood: STS-1 tokens are ERC-20s with mutable name(), symbol() and tokenURI(). Only the launchpad can call shift(), and only on a signed request from the creator, a schedule, a closed vote or the trending feed. Spec: https://shift-spl.xyz/docs
